Johannesburg Weather Recently

Johannesburg has experienced a rare, intense cold snap driven by a stationary cut-off low pressure system. This severe weather brought freezing temperatures, strong gusty winds, and even reports of light sleet or snow pellets to parts of the city and wider Gauteng region.

Local meteorologists advise residents to take precautions against low overnight temperatures and keep emergency contact details handy during severe weather warnings.
